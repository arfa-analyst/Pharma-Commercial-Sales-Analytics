# Pharma Commercial Sales & Revenue Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes pharmaceutical commercial sales data to understand revenue performance across products, regions, manufacturers, customer segments, and sales representatives.

The project demonstrates an end-to-end analytics workflow using Excel, SQL, and Power BI.

> **Dataset:** Synthetic/educational pharmaceutical sales dataset containing 1,000 transactions.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

To analyze pharmaceutical sales performance and identify the products, regions, customer segments, manufacturers, and sales representatives contributing to revenue, supporting data-driven commercial decision-making.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- **South America** is the leading revenue region at approximately **$6.25M (~24%)**.
- **Drug B** leads product performance with approximately **$5.77M (~22%)**.
- **Pharmacy** is the largest customer segment at approximately **$6.02M (~23%)**.
- Revenue is relatively distributed across products, with no single drug contributing more than approximately 23%.
- **James Smith** generated the highest individual sales-representative revenue at approximately **$80K**.
- Monthly revenue varied throughout the analysis period, with **August 2023** recording the highest monthly revenue.
